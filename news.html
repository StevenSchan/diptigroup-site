Professor Dipti Srinivasan Receives IEEE Transactions on Artificial Intelligence Outstanding Paper Award

Professor Dipti Srinivasan has received the Outstanding Paper Award from IEEE Transactions on Artificial Intelligence for the paper, "SpinalNet: Deep Neural Network with Gradual Input." The prestigious award is presented annually to recognise an exceptional paper published in the journal that has demonstrated outstanding technical quality and significant research impact after publication. The award was presented at the IEEE World Congress on Computational Intelligence (WCCI 2026), held in Maastricht, the Netherlands, in June 2026.
